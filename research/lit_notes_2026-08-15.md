# Literature notes, 2026-08-15

## Search run in parallel with W-INTL-258

The search target was the open method finding that the construction compares positions by one
effective BER although selection produces heterogeneous reliability. The implementation was
run in parallel with this search; no literature number was copied into the model.

### Hiller, Kurzinger and Sigl, 2020

*Review of error correction for PUFs and evaluation on state-of-the-art FPGAs*
([Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13389-020-00223-w)). Direct reading
confirmed the title, authors and year. The review says that reliability information helps
efficiency and gives dynamic RO counter values as an example. Tag: [supports]. It is a
methodological support, not evidence for the numerical values in `effective_ber_capacity.py`.

### Maringer and Hiller, 2025

*Information Theoretic Analysis of PUF-Based Tamper Protection*
([arXiv:2502.03221](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.03221)). Direct reading confirmed lower
bounds on achievable key rates and converse bounds on PUF-cell counts. Tag: [prior art].
The current work does not claim a new converse; W-INTL-251 through W-INTL-254 already record
the result as a reproduction in a different source and attacker model.

## What was not transferred

No finite-length decoder performance, word-failure probability, helper-data size, or hardware
area figure was imported from either source. The new model only evaluates explicit binary
symmetric channels and the scalar effective-BER proxy on the same synthetic populations.
