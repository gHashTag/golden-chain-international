# Literature note - W-INTL-275 - 2026-09-08

The literature search ran in parallel with implementation of
`research/generation_transition_control.py`. Titles, authors, and dates were
checked against the linked arXiv pages before the positioning was written.

## Prior art and positioning

- **It's a Feature, Not a Bug: Secure and Auditable State Rollback for Confidential Cloud Applications**, Quinn Burke, Anjo Vahldiek-Oberwagner, Michael Swift, and Patrick McDaniel, arXiv:2511.13641v1 (17 November 2025), [arXiv page](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.13641). The paper frames replay and rollback as stale-but-authentic state and discusses forward-only state continuity. This makes the present adjacent-generation rule a bounded reproduction/control of a known state-continuity boundary, not a new rollback protocol.

- **TEE is not a Healer: Rollback-Resistant Reliable Storage (Extended Version)**, Sadegh Keshavarzi, Gregory Chockler, and Alexey Gotsman, arXiv:2505.18648, [arXiv page](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18648). The paper separates rollback-resistant storage, crash-consistent recovery, and monotone state, including designs that do not require specialised hardware. W-INTL-275 measures only a pure byte-level transition check and does not inherit the paper's storage or distributed guarantees.

- **CRISP: Confidentiality, Rollback, and Integrity Storage Protection for Confidential Cloud-Native Computing**, Ardhi Putra Pratama Hartono, Andrey Brito, and Christof Fetzer, arXiv:2408.06822v2, [arXiv page](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.06822). The paper treats rollback of state saved on disk as a separate threat from runtime integrity. It supports keeping the repository's generation check distinct from claims about ordinary persistence.

- **Beyond Attestation: An Execution-Finality Architecture for Controlling Release and Limiting Unauthorized Extraction and Distillation of Sensitive OpenAI and Anthropic Claude Model Information**, S. Das and Sangam Das, [IETF draft](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/draft-das-rats-openai-anthropic-extraction-02). The draft states that rollback resistance is a property of protected state and that restoring ordinary software state must not recreate consumed authority. It is an architecture document, not evidence for this wire layout or its transition counts.

## Search result

The checked literature makes replay, state continuity, monotone state, and protected persistence established boundaries. The surviving contribution is narrower: a finite control around the repository's paired journal/anchor/commit bytes that accepts exactly the next generation and rejects replay, stale, skipped, ambiguous, tampered, and malformed candidates. [open conjecture] Persistent monotonicity, transaction atomicity, storage durability, power-loss ordering, distributed concurrency, security, hardware, and the G16 three-node shared-uplink demonstration remain unmeasured.
