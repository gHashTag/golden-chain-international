# Literature note — 2026-08-16

## Directly verified prior art

- **Maringer et al., “Analysis of Communication Channels Related to Physical Unclonable Functions,” arXiv:2112.02198.** Direct export.arxiv.org XML verification: https://export.arxiv.org/api/query?id_list=2112.02198. The abstract names a Varying Binary Symmetric Channel (VBSC) for unequal PUF-bit reliabilities and studies its capacity under channel-state information. This is direct prior art for the heterogeneous-capacity comparison in W-INTL-258.
- **Maringer and Hiller, “Information Theoretic Analysis of PUF-Based Tamper Protection,” arXiv:2502.03221.** Direct export.arxiv.org XML verification: https://export.arxiv.org/api/query?id_list=2502.03221. The abstract states finite-blocklength lower bounds and converse bounds on PUF-cell counts. W-INTL-251–254 already classify the project's bound as a reproduction in its own model.

## Consequence for this loop

The exact 15-fold repetition calculation in `research/heterogeneous_repetition_decoder.py` is therefore a finite-code reproduction/control under the VBSC framing, not a new channel theorem. It compares scalar mean-BER hard majority with a declared per-position weighted-LLR rule. [measured] At mean BER 0.06, the homogeneous control is equal below 1e-12; two heterogeneous mixtures produce lower exact word error for the weighted rule. [open conjecture] BCH performance, helper-data binding, and the area/metadata cost of retaining reliabilities are not established.

The search did not justify a stronger novelty claim. The next bounded experiment is the actual BCH decoder, not another capacity derivation.
