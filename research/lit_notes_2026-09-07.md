# Literature note - W-INTL-274 - 2026-09-07

The search ran in parallel with implementation of
`research/paired_anchor_commit_control.py`. Titles, authors, and versions were
checked by direct XML requests to
`https://export.arxiv.org/api/query?id_list=2608.00501` and
`https://export.arxiv.org/api/query?id_list=2505.18648`.

## Prior art and positioning

- **Machine-Checked Dual-Write Recovery from a Committed Log**, Andreas Andreakis,
  arXiv:2608.00501v4 (2026-08-13), [arXiv page](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00501).
  The paper formalizes recovery when related facts are durable in two independent
  systems, and studies generation/fence mechanisms and an atomic composite recovery
  step. This is direct prior art for the general dual-write recovery boundary.
  W-INTL-274 therefore does not claim a new dual-write protocol or theorem: its
  finite control checks one concrete parser that rejects mixed-generation
  `journal`/`anchor`/`commit` states under every partial-write order.

- **TEE is not a Healer: Rollback-Resistant Reliable Storage (Extended Version)**,
  Sadegh Keshavarzi, Gregory Chockler, Alexey Gotsman, arXiv:2505.18648v3,
  [arXiv page](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18648).
  The paper treats rollback-resistant reliable storage, failure-atomic changes,
  crash-consistent writes, and monotone incarnation numbers. It is prior art for
  separating rollback, crash consistency, and monotone state; it does not prove
  anything about this fixed three-component wire layout and does not make ordinary
  storage irreversible.

- **AWS Torn Write Prevention**, [AWS documentation](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AWSEC2/latest/UserGuide/storage-twp.html).
  This document describes incomplete-write protection at the block-storage layer
  and doublewrite recovery. It is an engineering boundary, not prior art for the
  finite parser control; filesystem and device semantics are not measured here.

## Search result

The prior art narrows the claim to a [measured] software-control result and makes
it a finite reproduction/control of a boundary, not a novelty claim for dual-write,
fencing, or rollback-resistant storage. [open conjecture] Crash atomicity,
device-backed monotonicity, durability, recovery after all three components roll
back together, distributed ordering, key management, leakage, side channels,
area/timing, FPGA integration, and the G16 three-node shared-uplink demonstration
remain unmeasured.
