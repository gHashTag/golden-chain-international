## Directly checked sources

- **Strieder, Frisch, and Pehl, “Machine Learning of Physical Unclonable Functions using Helper Data - Revealing a Pitfall in the Fuzzy Commitment Scheme,” IACR ePrint 2020/888.** The primary page is https://eprint.iacr.org/2020/888. It reports that public helper data, public challenges, and knowledge of the error-correcting code can expose machine-learnable features and labels; it discusses repetition and BCH constructions. Tag: [prior art/ threat]. This is a leakage and modelling-attack result, not evidence that the finite key equation below is secure.
- **Maringer and Hiller, “Information Theoretic Analysis of PUF-Based Tamper Protection,” arXiv:2502.03221.** Direct XML: https://export.arxiv.org/api/query?id_list=2502.03221; public abstract: https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.03221. The paper gives finite-blocklength and asymptotic key-rate lower bounds, converse bounds on PUF-cell counts, and a wiretap-coding helper-data construction. Tag: [prior art/ bound]. It does not supply the repository's BCH(127,57,11) binding experiment.
- **Maringer et al., “Analysis of Communication Channels Related to Physical Unclonable Functions,” arXiv:2112.02198.** Direct XML: https://export.arxiv.org/api/query?id_list=2112.02198; public abstract: https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.02198. It introduces the Varying Binary Symmetric Channel with unequal per-bit crossover probabilities and channel-state-information cases. Tag: [prior art]. The current result remains a finite-code control, not a new channel theorem.

## Consequence for W-INTL-262

The relevant prior art makes the boundary narrower, not broader: public helper data can leak structure in some PUF constructions, and information-theoretic work studies security with partial response/helper-data information. `helper_data_binding.py` therefore measures only the local key equation `K = S xor H(W)` for syndrome helper data. It must not be described as a security proof, a leakage bound, a resistance claim, or a replacement for the 2025 information-theoretic construction.

The literature search did not produce a directly checked 2025–2026 paper that reports this repository's exact finite BCH(127,57,11), 924-bit syndrome-helper, one-bit-helper-mutation control. That is a statement about this search scope, not a claim that no adjacent work exists.
