# Literature note — 2026-08-18

## Directly verified sources

- **Frank, Ponnapalli, Lomeli, Giridharan, Aguilera, and Crooks, “Real Life Is Uncertain. Consensus Should Be Too!”, arXiv:2602.11362.** Direct XML: https://export.arxiv.org/api/query?id_list=2602.11362. The abstract argues for probabilistic failure models and individual machine failure curves instead of a single fixed fault threshold. This is adjacent reliability methodology; it does not study PUF response-bit metadata or BCH decoding.
- **Bonomi, Farina, and Tixeuil, “On the Solvability of Byzantine-tolerant Reliable Communication in Dynamic Networks”, arXiv:2503.22452v4.** Direct XML: https://export.arxiv.org/api/query?id_list=2503.22452. The work studies delivery, integrity, and authorship under changing topology, bounded Byzantine faults, message loss, finite delay, and authenticated messages. It is an adjacent threat model, not evidence for the finite-code result.
- **Maringer et al., “Analysis of Communication Channels Related to Physical Unclonable Functions”, arXiv:2112.02198.** Direct XML: https://export.arxiv.org/api/query?id_list=2112.02198. Unequal per-bit crossover probabilities and decoder-side channel information are prior art. W-INTL-261 is therefore a finite BCH metadata-cost control, not a new channel theorem.

## Consequence for W-INTL-261

The contribution is deliberately narrow: `research/reliability_metadata_cost.py` quantises the
same per-position probabilities before the existing one-bit Chase list, holding BCH(127,57,11),
mean BER 0.060000, and the deterministic frame procedure fixed. At 200 frames per case, the
mild control changes 10 list failures at zero metadata bits to 5 at 127 metadata bits per word;
the split control changes 15 to 8. Two, three, and eight bits per position do not improve those
finite counts. This is [measured] and not an assertion about deployment.

[open conjecture] The metadata representation, helper-data binding, code-list size, area,
timing, and G16 hardware demonstration remain open. The search therefore narrows the next
engineering question to a costed metadata format rather than claiming a new reliability law.
